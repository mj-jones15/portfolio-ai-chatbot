Independent Study · University of Kentucky

Responsible AI Citation & Policy Influence Mapping

Advisor: Dr. Brent Harrison — Human-Centered AI, AI Ethics

Analyzed citation flows between RAI literature (AIES, NeurIPS) and 18 national AI governance frameworks, using a full NLP pipeline — Semantic Scholar API, e5-large-v2 embeddings, cosine similarity scoring — across a 20,000+ paper corpus.

  • Found a three-way misalignment: the largest share of RAI publications sits in fairness, the highest-cited papers are in explainability, and legislators most often reach for accountability language.
  • China's AI policy ranked highest in thematic alignment with RAI keywords, yet had zero supporting research abstracts above threshold — the widest gap between policy ambition and research grounding in the dataset.
