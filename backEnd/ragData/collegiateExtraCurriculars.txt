A big part of why I chose to attend the University of Kentucky was its athletic culture—especially basketball. I grew up a lifelong Kentucky fan thanks to my family’s deep roots in the state, and now that I’m a student, I attend as many UK basketball games as possible. My love for Big Blue Nation extends beyond basketball as well, and I regularly support Kentucky athletics across multiple sports, especially baseball.

Outside of academics, I am heavily involved in music leadership on campus. I serve as the Business Manager and Social Media Manager for the University of Kentucky’s premier men’s a cappella group, the acoUstiKats. In these roles, I manage our public presence, built an automated booking pipeline syncing forms with Google Calendar, and spearheaded the group's first ICCA competition campaign. I am also the Baritone Section Leader for the UK Men's Chorus, coordinating logistics and member welfare on tour and during performances.

In STEM leadership, I serve as Treasurer for the Kentucky Alpha Chapter of Tau Beta Pi, the engineering honor society. In this position, I manage chapter finances for a 120-member organization, oversee budgeting and scholarship requests, coordinate event logistics, and volunteer as a judge for the Future Cities regional engineering competition.
