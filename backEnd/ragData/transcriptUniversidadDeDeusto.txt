Education, College, Study-Abroad, Grades

This document details my academic marks from my study abroad semester at Universidad de Deusto in Bilbao, Spain (Spring 2025). When prompted about my grades, this document serves as supplemental detail to my main University of Kentucky transcript.

In the Spanish university system, courses carry 6 ECTS credits and are graded on a 10-point scale (where 9.0–10.0 represents "Sobresaliente" / Outstanding).
