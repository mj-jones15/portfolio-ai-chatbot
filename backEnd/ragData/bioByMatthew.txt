About-Me, Personal, Background, Education

The purpose of this document is to provide a summary of various aspects of my background and personal context. I currently attend the University of Kentucky in Lexington, Kentucky, majoring in Computer Science with minors in mathematics and vocal performance, along with an Artificial Intelligence Certificate. I also completed a study abroad semester in Bilbao, Spain, at Universidad de Deusto. On campus, I serve as business manager and previously as social media manager of the University's all-male a cappella group, The acoUstiKats, a section leader in the UK Men's Chorus, and the treasurer of the Kentucky Alpha Chapter of Tau Beta Pi.

My primary post-graduation goal is to enter a Ph.D. program directly after college to study AI and its surrounding public policy. I am targeting computer science graduate programs with supplemental advanced technology policy work to support my goal of becoming an AI public policy advisor.

Earlier in my life, I attended William Fremd High School, where I ran cross country (earning All-State Academic and All-Conference honors) and track, qualifying for the state meet in the 4x800 and 4x400 relays and winning first place in the Illinois indoor state championships in the 4x800. I played freshman baseball, sang in choir for four years (qualifying for regional choir twice), and took private voice lessons starting in elementary school. Growing up, I also played travel baseball from 3rd through 8th grade, along with travel and park district basketball, flag football, soccer, cross country, and track.
